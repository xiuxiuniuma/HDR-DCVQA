# HDR-DCVQA: HDR Display Content Video Quality Assessment

## Overview

This repository provides information and sample data associated with our research on HDR display content video quality assessment (HDR-DCVQA).

The proposed dataset was constructed by capturing HDR video content displayed on four consumer HDR devices under controlled acquisition conditions.

The dataset contains 200 display-captured HDR video clips derived from 10 original HDR video sources and four physical display devices.

## Display Devices

The following HDR display devices were used in the dataset construction:

- Sony A95L
- Hisense U8N
- LG B4 OLED
- TCL S551G

## Dataset Availability

A representative subset of the HDR display content dataset is provided for research and reference purposes.

The released materials include:

- Selected display-captured HDR video clips
- Corresponding subjective Mean Opinion Scores (MOS)
- Display device information

The released subset is intended to illustrate the characteristics of the dataset and is not the complete dataset used in the paper.
